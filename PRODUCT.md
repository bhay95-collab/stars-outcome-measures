# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Licensed rehabilitation clinicians using RehabMetrics IQ during patient assessment, documentation, progress review, and follow-up workflows on mobile and web.

## Product Purpose

RehabMetrics IQ gives clinicians a secure workspace for recording outcome measures, interpreting meaningful change, and maintaining patient progress records. Success means the interface stays out of the clinical task, protects patient data, and makes account and billing state clear without interrupting active care workflows.

## Brand Personality

Clinical, calm, and trustworthy. Copy should be direct and professional without sounding institutional or promotional.

## Anti-references

Avoid consumer wellness styling, decorative medical imagery, dense enterprise administration patterns, novelty interactions, and visual treatments that compete with clinical information.

## Design Principles

- Put the clinician's current task before product promotion.
- Use familiar native controls for authentication, billing, and account management.
- Explain consequential actions and failures in plain language.
- Keep clinical data legible and visually separate from account or commercial state.
- Preserve consistent behavior across mobile and web wherever the underlying account is shared.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support VoiceOver labels, minimum 44-point touch targets, reduced motion, sufficient contrast, dynamic screen sizes, and error states that do not rely on color alone.
